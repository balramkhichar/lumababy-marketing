Everything a night light needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

The app is free. Every sound, colour, animation, the timer, and the optional bedtime reminder work without paying.

A shape you can colour

Warm whites, peach, purple, a cloud, a fox, or make your own light from the palette and silhouette library.

Soothing sound

White noise and other steady tracks sit under the glow. Playback stays on your phone, even when the screen is dark.

Breathing glow

Solid, breathing, or fireplace. The animation is a slow pulse, not a toy show, built for a nursery at 2 a.m.

Sleep timer that fades

When time is up, the light fades to true black. Your brightness setting stays where you left it for tomorrow night.

Bedtime reminder

An optional local reminder on the days and time you choose. No account. No push server.

Private by design

Lights and settings live on the device. No analytics, no ads, no tracking across apps.

Privacy Policy

LumaBaby is a night light app for iPhone. It is built to work on your device. This policy describes what data is involved and what is not.

No account

LumaBaby does not offer sign-in. We do not create user profiles, and we do not operate a LumaBaby backend that stores your lights, settings, or usage.

What stays on your iPhone

Presets, brightness, sounds, the sleep timer, and settings are saved with Apple’s UserDefaults on the device so they survive a relaunch. That data does not leave the device through LumaBaby.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional bedtime reminder, iOS delivers a local notification at the time you chose. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Purchases

The optional Supporter subscription is available through the App Store. Apple processes payment and subscription status. LumaBaby only reads whether the subscription is active so it can unlock extra shapes. We do not receive your card details, and we do not use purchase history for advertising or tracking.

What we do not collect

LumaBaby does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporters. It does not track you across apps or websites. It does not sell personal information.

When you contact us

If you email support@fablestudio.app, we receive whatever you send (and the device details you include) so we can reply. We keep that correspondence only as long as needed to help you.

Children

LumaBaby is meant for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or ask a child for data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

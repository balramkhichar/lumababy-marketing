Subscription

Apple holds the keys.

Purchase, renew, and cancel through your Apple Account. In LumaBaby, open Settings → Subscription. On iPhone you can also go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

The night light stays free. Supporting us is only for more shapes.

New phone, same glow.

If you reinstall LumaBaby or switch devices, tap Restore on the Support screen in the app, or in Settings. Your Supporter status comes back with your Apple Account. Nothing else lives in the cloud.
